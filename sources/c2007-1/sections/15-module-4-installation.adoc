=== Module 4 - Installation

==== Scope

This module describes the methods for changing, wiring and installing Racons on buoys, structures, lighthouses and major floating aids. Applicable safety procedures from Module 2 will be reinforced during the lessons.

==== Learning objective

Upon completion, the student will have a satisfactory understanding as to how to properly and safely install Racons on aid to navigation structures.

==== Syllabus

===== Lesson 1 - Inspection prior to installation

. Physical condition.
. Documenting asset details.

===== Lesson 2 - Wiring

. Cable types.
. Connections.

===== Lesson 3 - Installation

. Physical requirements:
a. Racon orientation.
b. Protection of the Racon against site environmental conditions.
c. Working at heights.
. Installing Racons:
a. Racon stands.
b. Dissimilar materials contact.
c. Racon terminal boxes.
