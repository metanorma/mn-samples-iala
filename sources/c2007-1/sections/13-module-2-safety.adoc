=== Module 2 - Safety

==== Scope

This module describes methods for safely storing and handling Racons.

==== Learning objective

Upon completion, the student will have a satisfactory understanding of how to work with Racons safely.

==== Syllabus

===== Lesson 1 - Potential hazards

. Working with RF:
a. Dangers of exposure to RF.
b. First aid measures.
c. Dangerous voltages.
. Personal protection:
a. Climatic protection.
b. Body protection.
c. Hand and foot protection.
. Safe handling and storage:
a. Guidelines for transportation and storage.
b. Proper lifting methods.
