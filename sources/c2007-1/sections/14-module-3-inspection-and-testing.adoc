=== Module 3 - Inspection and testing

==== Scope

This module outlines the procedures for testing and transporting Racons. Applicable safety procedures from Module 2 will be reinforced during the lessons.

==== Learning objective

Upon completion, the student will have a satisfactory understanding as to how to properly and safely inspect, test, and troubleshoot problems with Racons.

==== Syllabus

===== Lesson 1 - Measurement and test equipment

. Cathode-ray oscilloscope (CRO).
. Power meter.
. Power sensor.
. Signal generator.
. Circulators.
. Racon test unit.
. Remote working radar.

===== Lesson 2 - Programming the Racon

. Testing Racon using remote ship's 3 and 9Ghz radar(s).
. Morse Code programming.

===== Lesson 3 - Transporting Racons

. Demonstrated competence in Racon shipment, including:
a. Racon shipment accessories required.
b. Racon packaging.
c. Racon shipment procedures.
