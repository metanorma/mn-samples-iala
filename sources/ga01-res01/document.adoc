THE ASSEMBLY,

RECALLING the transitional arrangements annexed to the Convention which agrees on the use of the General Regulations of the International Association of Marine Aids to Navigation and Lighthouse Authorities _mutatis mutandis_, unless the Organization has adopted General Regulations;

MINDFUL OF the provision of Article 7.7 (c) of the Convention which requires the General Assembly to approve the General and Financial Regulations of the Organization;

HAVING CONSIDERED the draft General Regulations and the Financial Regulations as agreed by the Conference in Tokyo from 7-9 November 2023:

. APPROVES the draft General Regulations and the Financial Regulation of the Organization;
. RESOLVES that they shall take effect immediately in line with the transitional arrangements annexed to the Convention.
