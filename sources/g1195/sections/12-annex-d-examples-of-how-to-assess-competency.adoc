== Examples of how to assess competency

The evaluation criteria for the competency testing of VTS communications is based on the framework outlined in IALA G1132, these include:

* Message Compilation
* Message Delivery
* Message Interpretation
* Standard Phraseology

For each evaluation criteria, the following examples may be used to assess the competency of VTS operators in the use of standard messaging structure and phrases. References to the relevant sections of IALA G1132 and IMO SMCP have been provided.

Further, these examples may also provide guidance to VTS operators on areas that may be assessed against during competency testing.
