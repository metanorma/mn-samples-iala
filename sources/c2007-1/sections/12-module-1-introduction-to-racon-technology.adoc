=== Module 1 - Introduction to Racon technology

==== Scope

This module provides an overview of Racon technology and the various AtoN applications.

==== Learning objective

Upon completion, the student will have a satisfactory understanding of how Racons work and be familiar with their various applications as an aid to navigation.

==== Syllabus

===== Lesson 1 - Racon technology

. Physical construction.
. Principles of operation.
. Terminology.

===== Lesson 2 - Racon types

. 9Ghz Band.
. 3Ghz Band.
. Frequency Agile.
. Advantages and disadvantages.
. Typical uses and applications.
. Racon detection by new technology (solid state) radar systems.
. Alternatives to Racon use.
